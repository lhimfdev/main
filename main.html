About

WHAT IS AN NFC CALLING CARD?

An NFC calling card is a business card with a small NFC (Near Field Communication) chip built in. When tapped on a compatible smartphone, it can open a link, such as your digital profile, so people can get your details without typing anything.

A chip inside

The card holds a tiny chip that stores a link. It needs no battery and no charging.

Tap to share

Hold the card near a compatible phone and the linked page can open, ready to save or follow.

A profile page

With LHIMF, the link leads to your one-page website with your contact details, social media and more, depending on your package.

Good to know

NFC support and settings vary by phone. Whether an app is needed depends on the card configuration and the receiving device.

FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

What is an NFC card?

A calling card with a small NFC chip inside. Tapped on a compatible phone, it can open a link such as your digital profile.

How does my LHIMF NFC card work?

The chip stores a link to your one-page profile website. Hold the card near a compatible phone and the page can open.

Do I need an app to use the card?

Often not, but it depends on the card configuration and the receiving device.

Can I customize the card design?

Yes. Share your design preferences in the order inquiry and LHIMF will confirm the details with you.

Can I update the information on my digital profile?

Updates depend on your package and arrangement with LHIMF. Contact us to ask about changing your details.

What is the difference between Basic, Personalized, and Advertising?

Basic has 2–4 links. Personalized adds 2–4 images and 4–7 links. Advertising has 4–8 images, an optional 30-second video and 8–10 links.

Can I use the card for my business?

Yes. Every package supports a personal or business profile.

Can I include my social media accounts?

Yes. Name, contact information and social media are included in every package.

How can I order?

Choose a package and submit the order inquiry form. No payment is collected on that form.

Which devices support NFC?

Many modern smartphones do, but support depends on the device, operating system and settings, and NFC may need to be turned on.
